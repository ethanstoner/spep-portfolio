Most of what I learned about myself at Great Oak, I learned by building. Late nights teaching myself Java to write a database from scratch. Training computer vision models to do things I didn't think were possible a year ago. Automation that runs for weeks without me touching it. None of it was assigned. I just wanted to see if I could.

What I'm taking with me from GOHS isn't any one project. It's the part of me that learned to sit with something hard and not look away. That's the person going into computer science next year, and the rest is still being figured out. I'm okay with that.
